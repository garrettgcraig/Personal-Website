Isle Royale — May 2024

May 23, 2024 · Isle Royale National Park, MI

Day 1 · May 23

Approx. distance: about 10 miles.

Observed: Western Rattlesnake Plantain, Wood Frog.

Day 2 · May 24

Approx. distance: about 4.5 miles.

Observed: Hackledmesh Weavers, kidneyleaf white violet, Scarlet Elfcup.

Day 3 · May 25

Approx. distance: about 5 miles.

Observed: fringed polygala, Virginia strawberry, Northern Cranesbill, Amygdaloideae, round-lobed hepatica.

Day 4 · May 26

Approx. distance: about 7 miles.

Observed: Dark Fishing Spider, Great Horned Owl, flowering plants, American Beaver, Cedar Waxwing, gray reindeer lichen, red columbine, tamarack, Common Loon.

Day 5 · May 27

Approx. distance: about 5 miles.

Observed: Moose, gold-and-brown rove beetle, Northern Azure, Waltzing Fly, Phormia, Orange-legged Furrow Bee, Winter tick.

Day 6 · May 28

Approx. distance: about 8 miles.

Observed: tamarack, meadow buttercup, Red Fox, Moose.

Day 7 · May 29

Approx. distance: about 9 miles.

Observed: Mallard, Red-eyed Vireo, thimbleberry, wild sarsaparilla, alternate-leaved dogwood, broad-leaved dock, showy mountain-ash, red baneberry, red-berried elder.
