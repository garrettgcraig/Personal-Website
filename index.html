About Me

Master's candidate in Environmental Science and Management at UC Santa Barbara's Bren School, studying conservation, environmental policy, climate resilience, and regenerative ways of living with nature. During non-desk hours, I'm often hiking and/or birding, giving far too much effort in beach volleyball, chuckling at clever crossword clues, or photographing neature.

Photography

Shooting with a camera is my longest-running hobby. It's outlived Lego set construction, Chicago sports fandom, Pokémon card collecting, and through-the-mail autograph hunting, among other pursuits that fell to the wayside years ago. My photos capture more than just scenes for me; they capture my feelings at the moment the shutter clicked.
