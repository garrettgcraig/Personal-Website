Projects

Interactive tools and applications built for exploring environmental data.

KnowKi

iPhone & Web iNaturalist API Spaced Recall

Learn to recognize the plants and animals around you by sight. Lessons come from research-grade iNaturalist observations near your chosen place, and spaced recall turns each species into kin. Includes a five-species Daily, trips to every U.S. national park, and Field Bingo to send you outside.

Launch KnowKi About the app

SeedFinder

Phenology iNaturalist API Native Seed

Given a place and a date, which native plants have collectible seed right now? Models seed-ripening windows from iNaturalist phenology annotations, with field photos of ripe fruit, handling notes, and a log that tracks each lot through stratification to germination.

Launch SeedFinder

Santa Barbara Wildlife Crossings

Road Ecology 3D Map SB 1250

Where should Caltrans build or retrofit wildlife crossings on Santa Barbara County's state highways? Combines community-science roadkill, culvert and bridge inventories, traffic, night stopping-sight distance and terrain to rank every half-kilometer of highway, with a 3D satellite tour of each type of solution.

Explore the Map

Public Lands Tracker

Leaflet Interactive Map Public Lands

Track your visits to America's wilderness areas, national parks, and national forests. Toggle layers, search by name, and watch your progress across 1,350+ public lands. Saves automatically in your browser.

iNat Rarity Explorer

R / Shiny iNaturalist API Biodiversity

Discover which of your iNaturalist observations are the rarest on Earth. Ranks every taxon you've logged by its global observation count, with an interactive lollipop chart and a sortable table linking back to each record.

Launch Explorer

ChronoSono

Party Game Phones Music

A music timeline party game for game nights. One device, a phone or a computer, plays each song through an ordinary Spotify link where nobody can see it, and each team places the song on its own phone's timeline by year. About 580 songs from 1954 to today, no accounts, no app to install.
