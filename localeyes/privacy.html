LocalEyes Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · Updated September 25, 2026 to describe anonymous usage totals

LocalEyes is an iPhone app made by Garrett Craig. It has no accounts, no advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking. This policy explains exactly what the app and its lesson service handle.

On your phone

Your progress stays on your device: which species you've practiced, your answers and review schedule, your Fieldbook, your settings, and your chosen place. You can delete it any time in Settings → Delete local progress, or by deleting the app. iOS device backups may include it, following your iOS settings.

Sent to the LocalEyes lesson service

To build a lesson, the app sends the following to the LocalEyes lesson service at api.garrettgcraig.com:

  • the center of your chosen place, rounded to about 1 km for searched places or about 11 km for approximate location, and your search radius;
  • the species group and whether you chose native or introduced species;
  • which species are due for review and which of their photos you saw recently, so lessons can repeat the right species with new photos;
  • your answers, requests for a different photo, and photo reports.

None of this includes your name, an account, a device or advertising identifier, or your precise location. The service does not log IP addresses, answers or locations.

To improve lessons, the service keeps anonymous daily totals: how many lessons were started and finished in each group, how many answers reached each question and how many were correct, how long finished lessons took (only in ranges such as 2–3 minutes), and how often photos were swapped or reported. These totals contain no identifier, place or individual answer, so they cannot be traced to you. The timing for a single lesson is deleted with the lesson after 24 hours. To limit abuse it keeps short-lived request counts in memory under a one-way hashed form of your network address; these are never written to disk. Lesson questions and area snapshots are deleted after 24 hours. Photo reports are kept so reported photos stay hidden.

To answer a lesson request, the service asks iNaturalist for species recorded in the rounded area. Those requests come from the service, not your phone, and contain no information about you.

The service runs on Fly.io, which carries its network traffic under its own privacy policy.

Location

Location is optional. The app asks for it only when you tap Use approximate location. It rounds the result to a coarse center and discards the precise fix, and it never tracks you in the background. You can always choose a place by name instead.

Apple services

Searching for a place sends your search text to Apple Maps. The app asks Apple for the nearest town name for the coarse center of your place, and for the state of places saved by earlier versions. Apple handles these under its own privacy policy.

Photos

Quiz photos load through the lesson service. Opening a photo full screen requests a larger copy the same way. Credit and source links open iNaturalist or Creative Commons in your browser.

Field guide links

For places in the United States, the Fieldbook, species pages and some lesson summaries show a link to a Bookshop.org list of field guides for your state. The app uses your chosen place's state to pick the list. Nothing is sent to Bookshop.org unless you tap a link. The lists belong to LocalEyes's Nature Books shop, so Bookshop.org credits purchases to that shop, and LocalEyes earns a commission. Bookshop.org handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

After your first lesson you can turn on a daily reminder. It is scheduled on your phone; LocalEyes has no push notification server.

Children

LocalEyes is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no accounts and collects no names or contact details from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
