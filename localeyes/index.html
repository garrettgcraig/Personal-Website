LocalEyes

Learn to recognize the birds, mammals, plants, herps and fish recorded near you, through short photo quizzes built from iNaturalist observations.

How it works

Choose a place, a search radius and a group. Each lesson is ten photos with four choices, drawn from research-grade iNaturalist observations recorded within that radius. Species you miss come back in your next lesson; species you get right come back after 1, 3 and 7 days. A species counts as learned once you recognize it in two different photos on two different days.

Common questions

A photo is too blurry or small to identify from.

Before you answer, tap Try a different photo to see another photo of the same species. The choices stay the same, so it doesn't give the answer away. If a photo seems wrong or inappropriate, email us (below) and we'll remove it.

It says there aren't enough species in my area.

Some places have few recorded observations for some groups, especially fish and herps. Try a larger radius, another group, or a nearby town.

Lessons won't load.

LocalEyes needs an internet connection to prepare lessons. Check your connection and tap Start again. Your progress is saved on your phone and is never lost when a lesson fails to load.

What does “Native species only” do?

It is on by default and limits lessons to species iNaturalist records as native where each photo was taken. Turn it off to practice introduced species instead. Introduced doesn't always mean invasive.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and tap Delete local progress. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. There is no account to close.

What are the field guide links?

In the United States, your Fieldbook links to a Bookshop.org list of field guides for your state. LocalEyes earns a commission on purchases through those links, which also supports independent bookstores.
