# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the local community around Coal Oil Point and Isla Vista (neighbors, surfers, beach walkers, birders, students) who want to know whether the Devereux Slough mouth is open, whether a breach is coming, and to see it happen. Secondary: visitors to Garrett G. Craig's portfolio site looking at it as an example of environmental data work.

## Product Purpose

A live page that answers two things together: the current state of the slough (mouth open or closed, water level, breach chance over the next 10 days) and how this seasonal lagoon works (dry in late summer, filling with fall and winter rain, full behind the sand berm, breaching, tidal for a while, closing again). Success: a visitor gets the status in seconds and leaves understanding the cycle.

## Positioning

The only public breach outlook for Devereux Slough: a model fit on 14 recorded breaches and measured water levels, fed live by the UC Davis / State Parks sensor, rain gauges, tides, and waves, with alerts when a breach becomes likely.

## Constraints and commitments

- Credit data partners clearly: CCBER (UC Santa Barbara), UC Davis Coastal Oceanography Group / California State Parks, NOAA, CoCoRaHS, Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Open-Meteo, NDBC.
- Accessible by default: screen-reader friendly, good contrast in light and dark themes, works on phones.
- Lives on garrettgcraig.com and uses the site's brand tokens (assets/css/brand.css).
- Copy: plain American English, no em dashes. Not an official forecast; uncertainty stays visible.
