DJ phone test

Three quick tests on the phone you'd use as the DJ phone. They show whether the no-API version of the game can work. Have Spotify installed and logged in.

1. Pick a song

In Spotify, open any song, tap Share, then Copy link, and paste it here. Or keep the sample.

2. Plain tap

Tap the button with the screen facing you. Then note what happens.

Open song in Spotify Try the spotify: version

Afterward, come back to this page and try the other button.

3. Face-down tap

This is how the DJ phone would work. Tap Start, then turn the phone so the screen faces the floor. You'll hear two beeps when it's ready. Then tap anywhere on the screen without looking.
