Herpetofauna Response to Ecological Restoration

Herpetology Ecological Restoration Coverboards

What long-term coverboard surveys can tell us about reptiles and amphibians returning to a regraded site.

  • Read More

Overview

Amphibians and reptiles are sensitive indicators of ecosystem health. This project uses long-term coverboard survey data from a UC Santa Barbara restoration site to look at how herpetofauna communities have responded since the site was graded.

Questions

  • How have reptile and amphibian detections under coverboards changed in the years since grading?
  • Which species returned first, and which are still scarce?
  • Can herps serve as practical indicators of restoration progress?

Status

Just getting started. I'm working through the long-term coverboard data now and will post methods and results as the analysis takes shape.
