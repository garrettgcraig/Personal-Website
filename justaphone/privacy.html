Just a Phone Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Just a Phone is an iPhone app made by Garrett Craig. It turns your Home Screen into a short list of words, shows your screen time, and can block apps you choose. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what it handles.

On your phone

Everything you set up stays on your iPhone: your launchers and the apps in them, widget styles and any photo you choose as a widget background, your nudge phrases, your setup progress and your Detox Mode schedules. The app and its widgets share this through an App Group on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. iOS device backups may include it, following your iOS settings.

Screen Time

Detox Mode and the screen time features use Apple's Screen Time framework, with your permission. The apps you pick to block are passed to iOS as private tokens that Just a Phone can't read, and your screen time and pickups are shown by an iOS report view that keeps the numbers away from the app itself. None of this leaves your phone or reaches the developer. You can turn off Screen Time access at any time in Settings → Screen Time.

Camera and unlock barcode

If you set an unlock item for Detox Mode, the camera is used only while you scan its barcode. The scan happens on your phone, no image is saved, and the barcode is stored on your phone only in scrambled (one-way hashed) form so the app can check it later.

Weather and location

Location is optional and used only for the Weather widget. If you turn it on, the app asks for your location while you're using it, never in the background. To get a forecast, your phone sends your latitude and longitude, rounded to about 1 km, to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Nothing else is sent with it, and Open-Meteo handles the request under its own privacy policy. Without location, the Weather widget is simply off.

Shortcuts

iOS has no direct link to open a few Apple apps, such as Phone and Clock, so the setup guide can install a Shortcut and a Shortcuts automation that open them. These run on your phone. Installing them opens an iCloud link to the Shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Opening other apps

When you tap an app in a launcher, Just a Phone asks iOS to open it. To show which apps from its directory are installed, it asks iOS whether certain apps can be opened; the answer stays on your phone.

Purchases

Any purchase in the app goes through Apple, including the monthly plan. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account; Apple confirms the purchase to the app on your phone. You can cancel the monthly plan anytime in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

Notifications

Any reminders are scheduled on your phone. Just a Phone has no push notification server.

Children

Just a Phone is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
