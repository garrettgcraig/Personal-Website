Just a Phone

Just a Phone is an iPhone app that replaces your wall of colorful app icons with a short, calm list of words, puts today's screen time on your Lock Screen, and can block the apps that eat your day. Quiet your phone. Quiet your mind.

Getting help

Email just.a.phone.support@gmail.com with what you were doing and what happened. Your iPhone model and iOS version help. I read every message.

Common questions

How do I put the launcher on my Home Screen?

Long-press an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Just a Phone, and add a Launcher widget. Swipe to the tallest size for a full-screen launcher. The setup guide in the app walks through this and the other steps.

Phone or Clock won't open from the launcher

iOS has no direct link to these apps, so Just a Phone opens them through Shortcuts. In the app, open the Open App Assistant and add both Shortcuts with their Get buttons. Then, in the Shortcuts app, go to the Automation tab, tap the automation for Just a Phone, and make sure its Automation switch is on.

I lost my unlock barcode

On the unlock screen, tap Can't find it?. After a 15-minute wait, Detox Mode unlocks without the barcode. You can then set a new unlock item.

My screen time shows zero or stopped updating

Open Just a Phone once. iOS sometimes pauses Screen Time tracking after an update or a reinstall, and opening the app restarts it. Also check that Just a Phone still has access in Settings → Screen Time.

How do I undo the setup?

Every step is a normal iPhone setting, so you can change it back in Settings at any time. Removing the widgets and deleting the app removes everything Just a Phone stored.
