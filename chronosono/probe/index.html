DJ phone test

Quick tests on the phone you'd use as the DJ phone. They show whether the no-API version of the game can work. Have Spotify installed and logged in.

1. Pick a song

In Spotify, open any song, tap Share, then Copy link, and paste it here. Or keep the sample.

2. Plain tap

Tap the button with the screen facing you. Then note what happens.

Open song in Spotify Try the spotify: version

Afterward, come back to this page and try the other button.

3. Face-down tap

This is how the DJ phone would work. Tap Start, then turn the phone so the screen faces the floor. You'll hear two beeps when it's ready. Then tap anywhere on the screen without looking.

Not started.

4. Hands-free timer

Tap a Start button, then turn the phone face down and leave it. After 2 seconds face down you'll hear two beeps, and the page tries to open the song by itself. Browsers often block this, so note what happens. Try both buttons.

Not started.

5. Hold and let go

Tap Allow motion first. Then press and hold one of the boxes. Keep your thumb on the screen and turn the phone face down. After 2 seconds face down you'll hear two beeps. Then lift your thumb, and the song should open.

Not started.

What to tell me

  1. Did Spotify open, or did the song open in the browser?
  2. Did the song start playing on its own, or did you have to press play?
  3. Did the face-down tap work?
  4. With the screen locked, does the lock screen show the song name?
  5. Test 4: with each button, did the song start by itself, open in the browser, ask you something, or do nothing? The line under the buttons says what the page saw.
  6. Test 5: with each button, did the song start when you lifted your thumb?

Also mention iPhone or Android, and whether you use Spotify Free or Premium.
