ChronoSono Play

How to play

A music timeline party game. Hear a song, then place it on your team's timeline by year. One device plays the music where nobody can see it, and each team plays on its own phone.

What you need

  • A DJ: one device with Spotify that plays the songs. A phone works face down; a computer works with its screen turned away from the teams.
  • Two to four teams, each with a phone. Nothing to install: everyone opens garrettgcraig.com/chronosono.
  • A speaker, or the DJ turned up loud.

Setting up

  1. On any device, choose Start a new game. It gets a four-letter room code; share it or the invite link.
  2. Everyone else opens the game, enters the code, and picks A team or The DJ.
  3. The device that started the game picks the years (1960 to today by default), the mode, the cards to win (10 by default, 5 to 20), and each team's starting tokens (2 by default). It can also leave out genres; a song stays in if it fits any genre you keep. An option evens out the decades so a wide range doesn't lean toward the busiest one.

A phone as the DJ

Turn on the motion sensor when asked. Each round, tap Next song, lay the phone flat, screen down, and wait for two beeps. Then tap the back of the screen, and the song starts in Spotify. Keep it face down until the team phones say it's safe.

A computer as the DJ

A device without a touch screen becomes a computer DJ on its own. Turn the screen away from the teams and click Next song; the song opens in the Spotify app, and you press play there. No Spotify app? Choose the browser tab option instead.

One-click play on a Mac

On a Mac, the DJ screen offers the ChronoSono DJ Shortcut. Download it, add it to Shortcuts, and turn on Allow Running Scripts in Shortcuts' Advanced settings. After that, Next song counts down from 3, plays the song in Spotify, and hides Spotify's window, even if another song was playing.

A turn

  1. 1The DJ plays a songNobody sees the title. If you know it, say the title and artist out loud before the reveal.
  2. 2Slot it inThe team whose turn it is taps the gap on its timeline where the song belongs, then locks it in.
  3. 3ChallengeThe other teams have 15 seconds. Think they're wrong? Claim a different gap for 1 token.
  4. 4The revealThe card flips on every team phone. Another team says whether the song was named.
  5. 5Keep, steal, or lose itPlaced right, you keep it. Placed wrong but a challenger got it right, they steal it. Otherwise nobody gets it.

Tokens

Each team holds up to 5.

  • +1Name itSay the title and artist before the reveal (not in Pro, where naming is required).
  • −1ChallengeClaim a different gap on another team's turn.
  • −1SkipSwap the song on your turn for a new one.
  • −3Trade for a cardBefore the song plays, trade 3 tokens for a free card placed for you. It uses your turn.

Modes

OriginalPlace each song in the right spot. Naming it earns a token.
ProThe card only counts if you also name the title and artist.
ExpertPlace it and call the exact year.

Winning

The first team to the target number of cards wins, the moment the card lands, even if it's a steal. The winner's timeline flips through on every phone, and the DJ gets a victory song to play. Play again with the same players from the end screen.

The songs

About 1,040 well-known songs from 1954 to today, each dated by the year it first came out, not a remaster or compilation. Each song has one or two genres: Pop, Rock, Hip-hop, R&B & Soul, Country, Dance & Electronic, Latin, and Folk & Singer-songwriter.

How it stays hidden

The DJ opens each song as an ordinary Spotify link, the same as tapping a link a friend sent you, while nobody can see the screen. The team phones never receive the song until the reveal. ChronoSono doesn't use the Spotify API, and it has no accounts and no ads.

Common questions

The song didn't play.

On the DJ, choose Song didn't start? Play it again. If a song won't play at all, a team can tap Song didn't play? to swap in a different one at no cost.

A phone DJ won't start the song when I tap it.

It has to lie flat, screen down; standing on its edge doesn't count. Wait for the two beeps, then tap. On an iPhone, allow motion access when asked. If you declined, reload the page and allow it.

Spotify shows the song on a phone DJ's lock screen.

Keep the DJ phone face down until the team phones say it's safe to turn it over.

On a computer, Spotify opens the song but doesn't play it.

Press play in Spotify. On a Mac, set up the one-click Shortcut from the DJ screen and it plays for you.

A team's phone died mid-game.

Open the game again with the room code and type the team's exact name to take its seat back. A new DJ device can join the same way and take over.

Can I turn my phone sideways?

Yes. On a phone turned sideways, your timeline runs left to right as a row of cards.
