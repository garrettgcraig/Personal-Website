# ChronoSono game screens

Scope: every screen at /chronosono/ (home, lobby, DJ phone, team phone, reveal, game over). Mode: Operate.
Audience: friends at a game night, dim living room, phones at arm's length. Job: know whose turn it is, place the song, challenge, see the reveal.
Constraints: answer hidden until reveal; standard controls; legible in a dark room; not busy, not childish, not the generic dark-card AI look.
Memorable moment: the reveal, when the blank title strip flips over and shows the song and year.
Logo (user's idea): a vinyl record drawn as a clock face with hands running backward, morphing through cassette, iPod, and a generic app tile. No real brand logos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The timeline is a jukebox title rack: each song is a printed paper strip with a colored top band and its year as the selection key. Refuses rounded dark cards with one glowing accent.
OWN-WORLD: Black lacquer ground (#121110), cream paper strips (#f4efe3) with one flat printed-ink band per decade (eight, 1950s to 2020s, the catalog's span); ink #1a1714 type on paper, white-on-black for chrome. Square corners, one-pixel rules, no glows, no gradients. Condensed bold grotesk for titles and keys, a readable text sans for body.
STORY: A player sees which team is up, sees their rack of strips, slots the blank strip between two years, locks in, and watches the strip flip to reveal the song.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: the animated logo at left of the wordmark, top. Below, two plain sections: join (room code as a cream key strip with a red band, Join beside it at the same height) and start (primary button). Team phone: brand and score bar on top, then the turn headline with one status line, then the rack of strips with empty strip holders as slots; Lock in, Skip, and Song didn't play? in a dock fixed at the bottom.
FORM: Jukebox title strips, own grounded list position 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key c1829194.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
